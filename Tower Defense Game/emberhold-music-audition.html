Melodic death metal built on Zelda's modal bones, voiced like a 16-bit console. Everything here is synthesised live in the page — no samples, no files — which is exactly how it will run inside the game. Play them, then tell me which survive.

Stingers

Two seconds each. In Zelda these do more work per note than anything else in the score — the reward phrase you hear a hundred times and never tire of.

What I need back. Which of the six to keep, which to cut, and whether the balance between guitars and melody is right. Rough reactions are more useful than precise ones — "3 is too busy" tells me plenty.

Each track loops seamlessly, so leave one running a while. A theme that survives three minutes on repeat is the one that belongs in a map you will replay for stars.

Tracks 2 and 3 would alternate across Greenwood, 4 and 5 across Frostgate, 6 on the two boss maps, 1 on the title and menus.
