Gamepad works too: ◻ attack, ✕ jump, triggers lock-on, hold LB + stick for orders.
Everything enemies drop is a crafting material, a potion or an ether. Press Tab to forge the next weapon tier (more power, slightly more reach) or armour tier (flat damage reduction), and to spend AP in the talent tree — Dash and the Whirl spin finisher both live there, along with the fourth and fifth combo hits. Bulwarks block anything from the front: go around them, or break the guard with a finisher. Cure spends your entire MP bar and locks it while it recharges. The score follows the fight, climbing when the field fills up. Levels, gear, materials and talents all persist: dying costs you the fight, not the grind.
