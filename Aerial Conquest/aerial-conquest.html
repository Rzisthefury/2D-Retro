Take back the Verdant Reach from the Umbral Dominion. On the map, attack any enemy node your land borders: villages pay gold, keeps and outposts weaken the castle behind them, and a castle takes its whole territory. Your castles turn gold into troops; send armies along the roads, recruit the Lords you beat as generals, and keep them loyal. Gold buys knight upgrades, first captures of castles and keeps pay skill points for talents, and the forge at any castle you hold turns gold and battle spoils into gear. Three save slots; beat Warlord Garrick Thorne for New Game+.
